# Korean SNOMED CT translation style guide (v5.1)

> v3-abbreviated + a narrow set of additions distilled from KHIS clinician
> feedback (May 2026): derivational-suffix preservation, contrast-modifier
> handling, the "symptomatic = diagnostic" radiology idiom, and explicit
> guidance against transliterating unfamiliar `-graphy / -gram` roots.
>
> Changes from v5: the rules that conflicted with the KR-extension's
> conventions (forcing solid imaging compounds like `자기공명영상`,
> forcing native-Korean body sites like `팔` / `다리` everywhere) have
> been **rolled back** — those choices are now framed as acceptable
> variation, not as a target to enforce.

---

# general

**Term length.** Maximum 255 characters, matching SNOMED CT specification.

**Script and case.** Korean terms are written in Hangul (한글). Latin
characters inside a Korean term (gene symbols, drug names, chemical
formulae, eponym
